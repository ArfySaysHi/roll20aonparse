Description

Few creatures are more feared than the lich. The pinnacle of necromantic art, the lich is a spellcaster who has chosen to shed his life as a method to cheat death by becoming undead.

The quest to become a lich is a lengthy one. While construction of the magical phylactery to contain the spellcaster’s soul is a critical component, a prospective lich must also learn the secrets of transferring his soul into the receptacle.

The Lich’s Phylactery

An integral part of becoming a lich is the creation of the phylactery in which the character stores his soul.

Each lich must create its own phylactery by using the Craft Wondrous Item feat.

Creating a Lich

“Lich” is an acquired template that can be added to any living creature.
